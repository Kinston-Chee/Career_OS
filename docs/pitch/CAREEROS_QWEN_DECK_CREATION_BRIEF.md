## Brief product introduction

CareerOS is an AI-assisted career operating system connecting three workspaces:

- **Students** preserve evidence from projects, internships, leadership, learning, and applications; understand what that evidence proves; identify career options and gaps; and take a visible next action.
- **Employers** discover early-career talent using evidence and context, preserve the reasoning behind candidate decisions, and reuse relationships from previous challenges, events, and hiring activity.
- **Universities** detect student-readiness and curriculum-market gaps, assign timely interventions, connect alumni and employer signals to action, and prepare reusable institutional evidence.

The shared product idea is:

`Signal → Evidence → Decision → Action → Learning`

CareerOS is more than a job portal, chatbot, or dashboard because it preserves context between important moments. It is designed to prevent useful student evidence, employer relationship history, and university insight from being lost before the next decision.

The current product is a polished multi-workspace prototype. It demonstrates many user interfaces and local workflows, but much of the data and intelligence is simulated. A live, permissioned evidence network across organisations is future-facing.
