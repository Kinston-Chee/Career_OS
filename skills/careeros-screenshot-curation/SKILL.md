# CareerOS Screenshot Curation

## Purpose

Select current product evidence proving CareerOS value and mechanisms across student, employer, and university workspaces. Optimise for explanatory value, not beauty alone.

## Required inputs

- Approved strategy or provisional slide jobs
- Current repository, routes, pages, components, visible copy, and mock data
- Runnable app, browser control, target aspect ratio, and intended placements

## Workflow

1. Start from slide jobs and proof needs, not attractive screens.
2. Trace each candidate from route to component and data source; record whether its state is default, query-driven, interaction-driven, or seeded.
3. Inspect through normal authentication and record viewport, workspace, route, tab, filters, scroll, modal/drawer, and reproducing interactions.
4. Reject legacy redirects, placeholders, broken/loading/error states, inconsistent variants, and conflicting copy or data.
5. Score stakeholder proof, mechanism visibility, narrative necessity, crop readability, truth/status clarity, and visual consistency.
6. Choose the minimum decisive captures.
7. Capture a stable desktop viewport without browser chrome, cursor, toast, incomplete assets, personal data, or secrets.
8. Preserve uncropped masters and create slide-specific crops retaining workspace context.
9. Plan concise annotations connecting evidence to mechanism; never fabricate absent functionality.
10. Approve the manifest before production and recapture after material UI/copy changes.

## Screenshot manifest schema

Record ID/filename, stakeholder, slide job, exact route, authentication, parameters, tab/filter/scroll/interaction state, source component/data, proof statement, demonstrated/simulated/future status, viewport/master path, crop, annotations, risks, recapture trigger, and approval.

## Required outputs

- Route/state inventory and rejected-screen log
- Approved manifest, uncropped masters, slide-ready crops, annotations, and recapture list

## Failure modes

- Beauty-first selection, undocumented states, or unverified old `pitch-deck/` assets
- Placeholder, legacy, broken, loading, or inconsistent screens
- Mock values presented as outcomes, contextless crops, tiny UI, or fabricated edits
- Deck production before manifest approval

## Final quality checklist

- [ ] Every screenshot maps to a slide job, proof statement, exact route, and reproducible state.
- [ ] All three workspaces have sufficient approved evidence.
- [ ] Legacy, broken, and inconsistent screens are excluded.
- [ ] Masters are clean, sharp, current, and chrome-free.
- [ ] Crops and annotations remain readable and truthful.
- [ ] Capability status and mock-data limitations are recorded.
- [ ] The manifest is approved before production.
