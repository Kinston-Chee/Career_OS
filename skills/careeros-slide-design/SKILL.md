# CareerOS Slide Design

## Purpose

Turn an approved CareerOS strategy into clear, credible, self-reading 16:9 slides using the product's blue-white-lavender language without copying dashboard density into the deck.

## Required inputs

- Approved outputs from `$careeros-deck-strategy` and `$careeros-screenshot-curation`
- Current CareerOS UI, approved brand assets, output format, and rendering toolchain

## Workflow

1. Set a consistent 16:9 safe area, grid, equal margins, title baseline, and footer/status placement.
2. Use white/near-white, deep blue text, blue/indigo actions, and restrained lavender accents with strong contrast.
3. Use at least 50 pt deck titles, 35 pt slide titles, 24 pt callout headers, and 16 pt body text. Shorten copy before shrinking type.
4. Give each slide one conclusion-led title, one takeaway, supporting proof, and brief interpretation.
5. Choose the simplest fitting layout: hero proof, split narrative/product, annotated screenshot, causal sequence, or comparison. Avoid dashboard-like card grids.
6. Prefer one large screenshot crop or two complementary crops; never use full-page thumbnails.
7. Crop to the exact feature state while retaining workspace context. Never stretch or distort.
8. Use one concise annotation pattern: numbered callouts, leader lines, highlight frames, or a focused zoom inset.
9. Align edges and baselines and use consistent spacing tokens.
10. Put causal logic, capability status, and implication on-slide; notes cannot carry essential meaning.
11. Inspect at full size and laptop-fit size. Enlarge, crop, split, or remove unreadable content.

## Information-density rule

Prefer a conclusion, 2-4 evidence points, and one primary visual. Split when hierarchy collapses, screenshots shrink, or takeaways compete.

## Required outputs

- Design specification covering grid, margins, palette, typography, spacing, and status labels
- Layout assignment, crop/annotation plan, master tokens, and design QA notes

## Failure modes

- Tiny screenshots, dense mosaics, excessive cards, pills, glass, gradients, glows, or ornaments
- Low contrast, competing headlines, essential notes-only context, inconsistent crops, or copy shrunk to rescue layouts

## Final quality checklist

- [ ] Every slide stands alone at 16:9.
- [ ] Titles do not wrap unexpectedly and type meets minimum sizes.
- [ ] Screenshots are large, sharp, cropped, and undistorted.
- [ ] Annotations explain mechanisms.
- [ ] Alignment, spacing, palette, and status labels are consistent.
- [ ] No slide resembles a compressed dashboard.
